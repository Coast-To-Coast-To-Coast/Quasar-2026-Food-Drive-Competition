      Good to know

      Questions

      Where does my donation go?

      Your donation goes to Food Banks Canada, which supports food banks in every community where we live and work, from coast to coast to coast.

      Do I get a tax receipt?

      Yes. Receipts come straight from Food Banks Canada, usually by email right after you give.

      How often do the totals update?

      Every Friday afternoon: December 4, December 11 and December 18. We’ll share the final total in early January.

      How does the draw work?

      Each time we reach a raffle stop on the journey, we draw one winner from everyone who has entered so far. You can enter by telling us you donated, by sharing a sweater photo, or both: one entry per person for each way to enter.

      Can my family and friends donate?

      Yes, please share the donation link with them. Every gift moves the truck. The raffle is just for our people, so only you can enter the draw.

      Can I enter the draw without donating?

      Yes. Send a photo of your favourite holiday sweater and you get one entry, no donation needed.

      Who can see the sweater photos?

      The photo book is part of this page, so anyone with the link can see it. Only send a photo you’re happy to share, and we’ll only show your first name and office.

      What does “$1 = 2 meals” mean?

      It’s Food Banks Canada’s meal metric: every $1 donated helps provide 2 meals. See how your gift helps

      Can I donate food instead of money?

      This drive is online only because it lets Food Banks Canada stretch every dollar, and it keeps our total simple. Food donations to your local food bank are always welcome too.
